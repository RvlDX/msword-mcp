# Chapter V Conclusions and Suggestions

## 5.1 Conclusions

Write one conclusion for each formulation question. Each conclusion holds the finding, its basis in Chapter IV, its scope condition, and the answer. Draw every conclusion from the earlier subchapters and the discussion results. Keep conclusions short and show how each one relates to the earlier discussion and its implication. Never introduce new facts and never claim unmeasured improvements.

## 5.2 Suggestions

Give actionable recommendations for the host organization. Cover the managerial aspect, the system aspect, and the further research aspect where they apply. Name the target role or unit, the action, the expected benefit, the prerequisite or risk, and whether the recommendation belongs to immediate implementation or future research. Prioritize by value and feasibility. Suggestions must follow from the problems and the conclusions.
