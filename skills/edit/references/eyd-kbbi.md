# EYD V and KBBI

Essentials for academic prose in Indonesian. Follow EYD V (Ejaan Yang Disempurnakan, fifth edition) and check word choice against KBBI (Kamus Besar Bahasa Indonesia). When the campus guide or the target journal states its own rule, that rule wins.

## Capitalization

- Capitalize the first word of a sentence and proper names of people, institutions, places, and documents.
- Capitalize Bab, Tabel, Gambar, and Lampiran when followed by a number (Bab III, Tabel 2.1, Gambar IV.1).
- Write academic degrees and titles as the guide asks, and leave academic titles out of the bibliography.

## Italics and foreign terms

- Write an untranslated foreign word in italics or underlined.
- Use the Indonesian term whenever one exists. Put the foreign term in parentheses right after the Indonesian term when an explanation is needed, or the other way around.
- Keep a foreign term that is standard in its field in its original form (see references/keep-it.md). Make sure it is italicized and consistent.

## Abbreviations and repeated words

- Use no informal abbreviations such as yg or thd. Write repeated words in full, for example kata-kata and not kata2.
- Write no unofficial abbreviations such as dll, dst, ybs, and dsb.
- Put a dot after an official abbreviation when the rule asks for it.
- Use the word atau instead of a slash, except in official abbreviations.

## Numbers and units

- Write numbers in words at the start of a sentence.
- Use a comma as the decimal mark in Indonesian text (0,03, not 0.03).
- Keep units attached to their numbers with the spacing the guide asks for, and keep the same unit system in the whole text.

## Punctuation

- Attach punctuation to the word it marks with no space before it. Write (data), sebutkan?, and "Judul" with the mark touching the word.
- Put a colon right after terdiri dari, adalah, sebagai berikut, yaitu, bahwa, diantaranya, and seperti. Use only one of them at a time.
- Use quotation marks for direct quotes and for titles inside the text. Never use quotation marks to mark emphasis.

## KBBI check workflow

1. List the doubtful words: standard versus nonstandard forms, absorbed terms, and affixed forms.
2. Check each one in KBBI. Keep the standard form. When the guide or the field keeps a nonstandard form, note the reason and stay consistent.
3. Check affixation: correct prefixes and suffixes, no melted consonants where the rule keeps them (dipengaruhi, mengkritik, memproduksi).
4. Report every changed word with its location, so the user can see what moved.
