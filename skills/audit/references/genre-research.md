# Genre module: research thesis, proposal, or dissertation

Load only for research documents. Follow the current program guide; do not assume every thesis or dissertation uses five chapters.

Check research gap, question, objectives, hypotheses (if relevant), concepts/variables, method, data, analysis, and conclusions for alignment. Literature should synthesize and compare relevant sources. Method must fit the question and available evidence. Address research ethics, consent, permission, anonymity, and data handling where relevant.

For proposals, distinguish planned methods, sample sizes, instruments, and expected outputs from completed research and results.

For quantitative research, verify population/sampling, instrument origin, validity/reliability, assumptions, and analysis fit. Dataset statistics must be reproducible from supplied data.

For qualitative research, verify design, paradigm/approach, informant criteria, recruitment, data collection, transcription/coding/analysis, and trustworthiness. Quotes/themes must trace to actual records.

For mixed methods, explain integration between strands. For R&D/prototyping, separate development model, design, implementation, and actual evaluation evidence. Never claim accuracy, usability, or effectiveness without valid testing.

Contribution must be proportionate to academic level and discipline; do not require theoretical novelty from applied work unless the guide requires it.

Traceability: gap → question → objective → theory/concept → method/data → analysis → result → conclusion → implication. Missing links are findings only when relevant to the document stage.
