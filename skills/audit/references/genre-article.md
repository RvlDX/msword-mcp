# Genre module: journal or conference article

Load for manuscripts intended for journals, conferences, or scholarly publication. Target venue instructions outrank generic IMRaD conventions.

Check target venue/scope, article type, audience, language, length, abstract, keywords, section order, reference style, tables/figures, and required declarations. If no target venue is chosen, report venue-specific checks as Not verifiable rather than inventing rules.

Use venue headings. Use IMRaD only when appropriate. Review, conceptual, case-report, protocol, and humanities articles may need another structure.

Check that gap and contribution are supported; methods are sufficiently transparent; results reflect actual findings; discussion distinguishes interpretation from results and avoids overclaiming; limitations are stated; and abstract/title match the body.

Ethics, consent, funding, conflicts, author contributions, data availability, acknowledgments, and supplements are included only when relevant or required.

Never fabricate peer-review status, acceptance, publication details, DOI, indexing, or journal metrics. Verify indexing claims with current authoritative evidence.
