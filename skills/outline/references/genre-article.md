# Genre: journal or conference article

Use this template for journal and conference manuscripts. Follow target venue instructions; do not use thesis chapter numbering unless the venue requires it.

## Front matter
- Title: state topic and contribution without overclaiming.
- Abstract: summarize purpose, method, actual main results when complete, and conclusion. For proposals/protocols, label planned work.
- Keywords: use accurate field terms.

## Main text
- Introduction: establish context, problem, relevant literature, gap, purpose, and contribution.
- Literature/background: include only when required or useful; synthesize relevant concepts and prior work.
- Methods: describe design, materials/data, participants or sources, procedure, and analysis.
- Results: report actual findings, not expected outcomes.
- Discussion: interpret findings, compare prior work, explain implications and limitations.
- Conclusion: answer the purpose and state only supported contributions.
- References: follow the venue's style.

## Conditional sections
Ethics, consent, funding, conflicts of interest, author contribution, data availability, acknowledgments, appendices, and supplementary material are included only when relevant or required.

## Checks
Identify article type (original research, review, conceptual, case report, protocol, or other) and target venue. Ensure each claim has evidence/source, methods fit the question, results are distinguished from expectations and interpretation, abstract/title match the body, and length/references follow venue rules.
