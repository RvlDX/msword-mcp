# Genre: Report (internship, KP, PKL, activity report, work report)

Use this template for internship and work reports. Fill the 14 variables in guide-adapter.md first. When the guide names a chapter differently, use the guide name and keep the job of the section the same.

## Outline template

For each section write the heading, the Job, 2 to 5 content points, the Evidence, and the Length hint.

### Chapter I Introduction

- 1.1 Background of the Problem. Job: state the facts of the problem at the host company, with at least one prior journal citation showing its journal name and ISSN or volume number.
- 1.2 Problem Identification. Job: list the main problems the background reflects, each as a statement the analysis can answer.
- 1.3 Scope and Limitations. Job: limit the scope and coverage of the problem discussed in Chapter IV and Chapter V, and leave room for further study.
- 1.4 Problem Formulation. Job: state the core problem as questions, restated from the identification and completed with the scope limits.
- 1.5 Objectives and Benefits. Job: explain the specific targets and the benefit for science or practice, in clear wording that leaves no room for misinterpretation. Note: some D3 outlines use the wording Objectives and Benefits of Research while S1 outlines use Objectives and Benefits of Writing. Follow the outline of the student program.
- 1.6 Writing Systematics. Job: describe each chapter with a short explanation of its material.

### Chapter II Literature Review

- 2.1 Literature Review. Job: present current primary sources from the last 5 years counted from the start of the research, and show how they direct the solution.
- 2.2 Related Research. Job: present related research from ISSN journals within the last 5 years, compared rather than listed.

### Chapter III Research Object

- 3.1 Brief History and Business Activities. Job: describe the company history and business activities from official host-approved information.
- 3.2 Organization Structure. Job: draw the organization structure and show the position of the studied system and the related units.
- 3.3 Authority and Responsibility. Job: explain the authority and responsibility of each organization element according to the structure, separating documented responsibilities from observed practice.

### Chapter IV Running System Analysis

- 4.1 General Overview. Job: describe the general picture of the running system and a short description of how it runs.
- 4.2 System Description. Job: describe the running system in full, covering every process it executes, with diagrams matched to the problem. Use a document flowmap or flowchart for data procedures, a topology or architecture diagram for networks, and an IoT architecture diagram for IoT.
- 4.3 System Problems. Job: analyze the problem in four stages: understand the problem area, analyze the problem and the opportunity, analyze the business process, and set the goals of the system improvement. Use one standardized method: PIECES, SWOT, or Information Characteristics. Never fill a category without evidence. A category can be marked no evidence found yet or out of scope.
- 4.4 Proposed System. Job: present the global design of the proposed system, matched to the analysis and to the study program, with diagrams the program asks for. Link each proposed feature to one or more evidenced findings. Discuss feasibility, dependencies, risks, and migration or training where material.

### Chapter V Conclusions and Suggestions

- 5.1 Conclusions. Job: state the important findings from the discussion as general or specific statements, drawn from the earlier subchapters.
- 5.2 Suggestions. Job: give development suggestions for the host organization, covering the managerial aspect, the system aspect, and the further research aspect.

## Program note

For IT programs the Chapter IV focus differs by field: business systems and IT audit, computer networks, IoT, security, or AI, mobile and game programming, or web and desktop programming. Match the proposed system to the program focus and flag a mismatch for supervisor confirmation.
